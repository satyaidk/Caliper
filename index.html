From model to print in three steps

  1. Open Drop a model onto the plate. Measure it, stand it on a face, or arrange several parts.
  2. Slice Pick the filament, plate and quality. Caliper uses Bambu’s own P1S profiles and checks every move before it lets you download.
  3. Print Copy the .gcode.3mf to the printer’s microSD card and start it from the screen. Watch the first layer.

Single filament on the P1S with a 0.4 mm nozzle. Caliper is new: supervise your first prints.

Install it as an app

It opens in its own window, works offline once loaded, and opens model files from your desktop.

Use it in the browser

In Chrome or Edge, use the install icon at the end of the address bar. On an iPhone or iPad, open the Share menu in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen.
